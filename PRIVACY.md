# Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

This policy covers this unofficial, fan-made Windows runtime for Metroid Prime 4: Beyond ("the software") and its Discord application. The project has no accounts and runs no servers of its own, so it does not collect your personal data.

## What stays on your PC

Settings, saves, logs, screenshots, recordings and traces are stored in the game's folder on your own PC. The software does not upload them. If you choose to share one, for example in a bug report, you control what you share.

## What leaves your PC

**Discord status.** If Discord is open and a Discord application ID is set, the software tells the Discord app on your PC to show a status. It sends the application ID, the software's process ID, the status text, the artwork name and the time you started playing. Discord then shows that status under its own [Privacy Policy](https://discord.com/privacy). The software does not read your Discord messages, friends or account details. Set `discord=0` in the `.ini` file to turn this off.

**Update checks.** The launcher can contact GitHub to check for and download updates. GitHub receives the usual request details, such as your IP address, under its own [privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

**Button icon packs.** If you choose to download icon packs, the software contacts the host of the pack, such as GameBanana. That host receives the usual request details under its own policy.

**Bug reports.** The launcher's bug report option opens a GitHub page in your browser, with details such as the version number filled in. Nothing is sent unless you submit that page yourself.

## What we do not do

The software has no analytics, advertising or tracking. We do not sell or share personal data, because we do not collect it.

## Children

The software is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

## Changes and questions

Changes to this policy will be made in this file. For questions, open an issue on [the project's GitHub repository](https://github.com/PlagueDrRiess/salsburysteaks/issues).
